Featured Project

Personal Project · Unity · Steam

Co-Creator & Creative Director

A cozy top-down action roguelike about cultivating plants on your ship between combat runs. Built the brand and visual identity from scratch, owning all 3D models, level design, VFX and characters through to logo design and Steam page key art cuts.

  • Player. Modelled from the first blockout to the in-engine hero, with a silhouette that stays readable from the top-down camera.
  • Procedural levels. Modelled modular environment tilesets that assemble at runtime, with editor tools so nobody hand-places a room.
  • Enemies. Built as one readable voxel family: potato bug, grubs, mosquitoes, and more, sharing a palette and visual design rules.
  • Plants. Modelled plants as five-stage families from seedling through vegetative, mature, and verdant, until each one blooms into a Hyperflora.
  • An AI-assisted workflow. Project skills, reference docs, and the Unity editor driven over MCP, all built in-house. The art stays handmade. The tools are how a small team moves this fast.
